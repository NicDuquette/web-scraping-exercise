Recreation Supervisor

City of Meadowbrook Parks and Recreation Department

250 Lakeside Boulevard
Meadowbrook, TX 75021

Job Details

Salary Range $54,700 - $69,600 annually
Job Type Full-time
Work Setting In-person
Benefits Medical, dental, and vision coverage; city retirement plan; paid vacation and sick leave; 12 paid holidays; recreation-center membership; employee wellness program; and training reimbursement.
Full Job Description

The City of Meadowbrook Parks and Recreation Department is seeking a Recreation Supervisor to oversee community recreation programs, seasonal activities, and facility operations. The position supervises program staff and part-time instructors and helps ensure that programs are safe, welcoming, accessible, and financially sustainable.

Responsibilities include planning seasonal program schedules, hiring and training part-time staff, managing program registrations and budgets, coordinating facility use, responding to participant questions, and evaluating program participation. The supervisor also works with schools, neighborhood groups, and community organizations to develop new activities.

Candidates should have experience coordinating staff or community programs and strong customer-service skills. Familiarity with recreation programming, youth services, facility operations, or event planning is preferred. Evening and weekend availability is an important part of the position.

A bachelor's degree in parks and recreation, public administration, education, hospitality, or a related field is preferred. Two years of experience in recreation programming, community services, or a related setting is desirable. Equivalent combinations of education and experience may be considered.

The Parks and Recreation Department operates multiple recreation centers, sports fields, parks, and community programs. The work is practical and public-facing, and supervisors are expected to spend substantial time in program locations rather than at a desk.

This is an in-person position. The regular schedule includes several evenings each week and occasional weekends, particularly during sports seasons, festivals, and special events. Schedule adjustments are provided when evening or weekend assignments are required.

To apply, submit the City application, resume, and cover letter. Selected applicants will participate in an interview and may be asked to respond to a short scenario involving a community-program or facility issue.

The City of Meadowbrook is an equal opportunity employer and complies with applicable accessibility and nondiscrimination requirements.
