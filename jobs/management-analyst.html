Management Analyst

State Department of Administrative Services

101 Capitol Mall
Riverton, IL 62701

Job Details

Salary Range $76,100 - $99,800 annually
Job Type Full-time
Work Setting Remote-friendly hybrid
Benefits State employee retirement plan; medical, dental, and vision coverage; paid vacation and sick leave; 12 holidays; commuter benefit; student loan assistance program; and professional development reimbursement.
Full Job Description

The State Department of Administrative Services is seeking a Management Analyst to support organizational improvement projects across state government. The analyst will conduct research, analyze administrative processes, and develop recommendations intended to improve service delivery, efficiency, and accountability.

Projects may involve program reviews, workforce analysis, procurement processes, customer-service improvements, and implementation of new administrative systems. Analysts gather information through interviews and document review, analyze operational data, prepare findings, and present recommendations to agency leadership.

Candidates should be strong analytical thinkers with excellent writing and presentation skills. Familiarity with process mapping, survey design, spreadsheets, or statistical analysis is preferred. Experience using Python, R, SQL, or another programming language for data analysis is desirable but not required.

A bachelor's degree in public administration, public policy, business, economics, sociology, statistics, or a related field is required. Two years of analytical, consulting, policy, or program-management experience is preferred. Relevant graduate study may substitute for some experience.

The Department maintains a project-based work environment in which analysts may support several initiatives at once. Work requires frequent interaction with managers and front-line employees across different state agencies and the ability to communicate technical findings to nontechnical audiences.

Employees generally work in a hybrid arrangement, with the specific schedule determined by the project team. Analysts may work remotely several days each week but are expected to attend important meetings and workshops in person when requested. Statewide travel is occasional.

To apply, submit an online application, resume, and cover letter. Candidates may complete a short analytical exercise and structured interview. Applicants should be prepared to discuss a project in which they used evidence to improve a process or inform a decision.

The State Department of Administrative Services is an equal opportunity employer and participates in the state's accessibility and workplace accommodation programs.
