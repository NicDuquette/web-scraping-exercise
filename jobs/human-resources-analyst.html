Human Resources Analyst

City of Pine Ridge

80 Municipal Drive
Pine Ridge, OR 97045

Job Details

Salary Range $69,800 - $90,500 annually
Job Type Full-time
Work Setting In-person
Benefits Medical, dental, and vision coverage; PERS retirement; paid vacation and sick leave; 12 paid holidays; life and disability insurance; wellness reimbursement; and professional development funds.
Full Job Description

The City of Pine Ridge is seeking a Human Resources Analyst to join its central Human Resources Department. The position provides analytical and administrative support for classification and compensation, recruitment, employee records, and workforce planning across the City organization.

The analyst will prepare compensation analyses, maintain personnel reports, assist with salary surveys, coordinate recruitment processes, and respond to questions from employees and department managers. The position may also support labor-relations research, policy updates, and special projects involving workforce data.

Strong attention to detail and discretion with confidential information are essential. Candidates should be comfortable organizing data, preparing clear written materials, and explaining administrative policies to employees. Experience with spreadsheets and human-resources information systems is preferred; experience with SQL or other data tools is a plus.

A bachelor's degree in human resources, public administration, business administration, economics, psychology, or a related field is required. Two years of professional experience in human resources, compensation, personnel administration, or a related analytical role is preferred.

The Human Resources Department serves approximately 1,100 City employees across public safety, public works, community services, utilities, and administrative departments. Staff work in a structured environment with established procedures while regularly responding to new questions and time-sensitive requests.

This position is based at City Hall and is primarily in-person. Occasional early-morning, evening, or weekend work may be required during hiring events, employee orientations, or other scheduled activities.

To apply, submit the online application, resume, and cover letter. Candidates selected for further consideration will complete a structured interview and may be asked to review a sample workforce-data exercise.

The City of Pine Ridge is an equal opportunity employer and provides reasonable accommodations to qualified applicants during the recruitment process.
