Full Job Description

The City of Arroyo Vista is seeking a Budget Analyst to join its Finance Department. The Budget Analyst performs professional financial and administrative analysis in support of the City's annual operating and capital budgets and provides analytical support to City departments throughout the fiscal year.

Primary responsibilities include reviewing departmental budget requests, analyzing expenditure and revenue trends, preparing financial forecasts, monitoring budget-to-actual performance, and assisting with the preparation of the City's proposed and adopted budget documents. The Budget Analyst will also conduct research on fiscal and management issues and prepare reports and presentations for department directors, the City Manager, and the City Council.

The successful candidate will be comfortable working with large financial datasets and translating quantitative information into clear recommendations for nontechnical audiences. The position requires regular use of spreadsheet software and the City's financial management system. Experience with data visualization, statistical software, or other analytical tools is welcomed but is not required.

The position requires regular use of spreadsheet software and the City's financial management system. Experience using Python for data analysis or automation is desired but not required. Familiarity with data visualization, statistical software, or other analytical tools is also welcomed.

The Finance Department operates in a collaborative environment and works closely with staff throughout the organization. The Budget Analyst should be able to manage multiple assignments, meet firm deadlines during the annual budget cycle, exercise sound judgment, and communicate effectively in writing and in person.

This position follows a hybrid work schedule. Employees are expected to work in person at City Hall at least three days per week, with remote work permitted on designated days subject to departmental needs. Additional in-person attendance may be required during budget preparation, public hearings, and other high-demand periods.

To apply, submit an online application, resume, and brief cover letter describing your relevant analytical and budgeting experience. Candidates whose qualifications most closely match the City's needs may be invited to participate in a written exercise and panel interview. Applications will be reviewed as they are received.

The City of Arroyo Vista is an equal opportunity employer and is committed to providing a workplace free from unlawful discrimination and harassment. Reasonable accommodations are available to qualified applicants with disabilities upon request.
