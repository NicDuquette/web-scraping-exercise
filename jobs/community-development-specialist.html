Community Development Specialist

Town of Cedar Glen

15 Main Street
Cedar Glen, CO 80421

Job Details

Salary Range $63,900 - $82,750 annually
Job Type Full-time
Work Setting Hybrid - one remote day per week
Benefits Medical, dental, and vision insurance; Colorado PERA retirement; paid vacation and sick leave; 11 paid holidays; transit pass; and annual training stipend.
Full Job Description

The Town of Cedar Glen is hiring a Community Development Specialist to support neighborhood improvement, affordable housing, economic-development, and community grant programs. The position works closely with residents, nonprofit organizations, local businesses, and other Town departments.

Responsibilities include reviewing grant applications, tracking project milestones, preparing staff reports, maintaining program records, assisting with community meetings, and conducting research on housing and economic-development issues. The specialist may also help administer small business assistance programs and neighborhood improvement initiatives.

The ideal candidate will have strong organizational and communication skills and an interest in local government and community development. Knowledge of housing policy, grant administration, zoning, or economic development is preferred but not required. Familiarity with spreadsheets and basic data analysis is helpful.

A bachelor's degree in public administration, urban planning, public policy, sociology, economics, community development, or a related field is required. Relevant internships or one to three years of professional experience are preferred.

Cedar Glen is a growing mountain community with a small municipal staff. Employees are expected to be willing to work across program areas and to interact regularly with residents. The position combines desk work with community meetings, site visits, and occasional fieldwork.

The position is based at Town Hall and permits one remote work day per week after an initial training period. Evening attendance at public meetings and community workshops is occasionally required, generally with advance notice.

Applicants should submit a resume, cover letter, and application through the Town website. Candidates may be asked to complete a brief written exercise involving a fictional community grant or development proposal.

The Town of Cedar Glen is an equal opportunity employer and encourages applications from candidates with a variety of professional and lived experiences.
