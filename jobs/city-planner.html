City Planner

100 Civic Plaza

Job Details

Salary Range $82,000 – $108,000 per year
Job Type Full-time
Work Setting Hybrid
Benefits Medical, dental, and vision insurance; participation in the city retirement plan; paid vacation, holidays, and sick leave; flexible spending account; and a professional development allowance.
Full Job Description

The City of Riverton is seeking a City Planner to join the Community Development Department. The City Planner will help guide the physical development of Riverton by reviewing development proposals, preparing planning reports, supporting long-range planning projects, and providing information to residents, applicants, and elected officials.

The position works closely with planners, engineers, public works staff, legal staff, neighborhood organizations, developers, and members of the public. Responsibilities include reviewing applications for consistency with the municipal code and adopted plans; preparing staff reports and recommendations; conducting site visits; maintaining planning records; and presenting projects at public meetings. The planner may also assist with updates to the city's General Plan, zoning regulations, and transportation and housing programs.

Successful candidates will be comfortable interpreting regulations, analyzing land-use and demographic information, writing clear reports, and explaining technical issues to people with a wide range of backgrounds. The City is especially interested in candidates who can work collaboratively and who are interested in the practical challenges of balancing growth, housing, transportation, neighborhood character, and public services.

A bachelor's degree in urban planning, public policy, geography, or a related field is required. One to three years of professional or internship experience in planning, local government, or a related field is preferred. Candidates should be familiar with Microsoft Office or comparable productivity software. Experience with geographic information systems (GIS), zoning codes, or public meeting procedures is helpful but not required.

This is a hybrid position. Employees generally work in the Civic Plaza office three days each week and may work remotely on other scheduled days. Because the position includes site visits and public meetings, occasional evening meetings and visits to development sites are required.

To apply, submit a resume and cover letter through the Public Careers Hub application portal. Applications will be reviewed beginning September 29. The recruitment may close at any time once a sufficient number of qualified applications have been received.
