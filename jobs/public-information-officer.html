Public Information Officer

County of Mesa Vista

600 Government Center Way
Mesa Vista, AZ 85211

Job Details

Salary Range $74,300 - $96,700 annually
Job Type Full-time
Work Setting Hybrid - three days in office
Benefits Medical, dental, and vision insurance; county retirement plan; paid holidays, vacation, and sick leave; commuter benefit; employee wellness program; and tuition assistance.
Full Job Description

The County of Mesa Vista is seeking a Public Information Officer to support communications for the County Executive Office. The position develops clear, timely public information about County programs, services, initiatives, and emergency operations and works with departments throughout the organization to coordinate consistent messaging.

Responsibilities include drafting news releases, website content, newsletters, public notices, talking points, and social media posts; responding to media inquiries; coordinating public meetings and press events; and maintaining communication calendars. The Public Information Officer will also help prepare communications during severe weather, infrastructure disruptions, and other time-sensitive events.

Candidates should be excellent writers and editors who can explain complex public programs in accessible language. Experience with content-management systems, basic photography or video, email newsletters, and social media platforms is preferred. The ability to work calmly under deadline pressure and exercise good judgment about public information is essential.

A bachelor's degree in communications, journalism, public relations, public administration, or a related field is required, along with at least three years of professional communications experience. Experience working for a public agency or other highly regulated organization is helpful but not required.

The County's communications team is small and highly collaborative. Staff work with elected officials, department directors, program managers, and subject-matter experts and must be comfortable receiving feedback from multiple stakeholders while maintaining clear editorial standards.

This is a hybrid position with at least three days per week in the County Government Center. Attendance at evening public meetings, press conferences, or emergency operations may occasionally be required, and the successful candidate must be available for occasional after-hours response.

Applicants should submit a resume, cover letter, and two or three writing samples, preferably demonstrating the ability to communicate technical or policy information to a general audience. Selected candidates will participate in a writing exercise and structured interview.

The County of Mesa Vista is an equal opportunity employer. The County is committed to accessibility and will provide reasonable accommodations throughout the recruitment process.
