Transportation Planner

Central Coast Metropolitan Planning Organization

220 Harbor Avenue, Suite 300
Santa Marina, CA 93108

Job Details

Salary Range $82,600 - $109,400 annually
Job Type Full-time
Work Setting Hybrid - flexible schedule
Benefits Medical, dental, and vision insurance; pension plan; generous paid leave; 12 paid holidays; commuter assistance; professional certification reimbursement; and flexible spending accounts.
Full Job Description

The Central Coast Metropolitan Planning Organization is hiring a Transportation Planner to support regional planning studies and long-range transportation programs. The planner will work with city and county transportation departments, transit agencies, state officials, and community organizations to improve mobility and coordinate investments across the region.

Typical assignments include analyzing traffic and travel data, preparing maps and tables, evaluating transportation projects, assisting with grant applications, and drafting technical memoranda and planning documents. The position will also support public outreach and help organize meetings of technical advisory committees and regional planning groups.

Candidates should have knowledge of transportation planning principles, land-use planning, or public policy and be comfortable working with data. Experience with GIS, spreadsheets, or statistical software is preferred. Familiarity with federal transportation planning requirements is an advantage but is not required for entry-level candidates.

A bachelor's degree in urban or regional planning, civil engineering, geography, public policy, transportation, economics, or a related discipline is required. Two years of relevant professional or internship experience is preferred. A master's degree may substitute for some professional experience.

The organization employs a multidisciplinary staff of planners, engineers, analysts, and public-engagement specialists. Work is generally office-based but may include site visits, public meetings, transit rides, and occasional evening events. The successful candidate should be able to balance independent analytical work with frequent collaboration.

The regular work schedule is hybrid and can be adjusted within agency guidelines. Travel throughout the Central Coast is expected several times each month, with occasional overnight travel for regional or state meetings.

To apply, submit an application, resume, and cover letter. A writing sample is encouraged but not required. Candidates may be asked to complete a short data-analysis or planning exercise during the hiring process.

The Central Coast Metropolitan Planning Organization is an equal opportunity employer and welcomes qualified applicants with varied academic, professional, and community backgrounds.
