Environmental Program Manager

North Valley Regional Conservation Authority

1250 Riverfront Drive
Fairview, CA 95618

Job Details

Salary Range $91,200 - $118,900 annually
Job Type Full-time
Work Setting In-person
Benefits CalPERS retirement; medical, dental, and vision coverage; paid vacation and sick leave; 13 paid holidays; deferred compensation program; mileage reimbursement; and annual professional development allowance.
Full Job Description

The North Valley Regional Conservation Authority is seeking an Environmental Program Manager to oversee programs that improve watershed health, habitat connectivity, and community resilience. This position works across agency departments, local governments, nonprofit partners, and state and federal resource agencies.

The manager will develop annual work plans, coordinate grant-funded projects, track program budgets and milestones, and supervise a small team of environmental specialists and project coordinators. The position also prepares staff reports and presentations for the Authority Board and represents the organization at regional meetings and public events.

Successful candidates will have experience managing environmental or natural-resource programs and working with multiple public and private partners. Knowledge of watershed management, conservation planning, environmental permitting, or climate adaptation is preferred. Strong project-management, writing, and public-speaking skills are required.

A bachelor's degree in environmental science, natural resources, public administration, planning, or a related field is required. A master's degree is desirable but not required. At least five years of progressively responsible experience in environmental programs, resource management, grants administration, or a related field is preferred.

The Authority is a small regional organization with a practical, collaborative culture. Staff frequently move between project planning, field visits, community meetings, and administrative work. The position may occasionally require evening meetings and local travel, including visits to conservation sites and construction projects.

The position is primarily based at the Authority's Fairview office and is in-person. Regular travel within the North Valley region is expected, and occasional travel elsewhere in the state may be required for conferences, training, or meetings with partner agencies.

To apply, submit the online application, resume, and a statement describing your experience managing environmental programs and partnerships. First-round interviews may be conducted by videoconference, followed by an in-person panel interview. The position will remain open until filled.

The North Valley Regional Conservation Authority provides equal employment opportunities to all qualified applicants and encourages candidates from a broad range of professional and educational backgrounds to apply.
