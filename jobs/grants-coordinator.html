Grants Coordinator

Regional Housing Partnership

310 Market Street, Suite 200
River City, MO 63103

Job Details

Salary Range $58,400 - $74,900 annually
Job Type Full-time
Work Setting Hybrid - two remote days per week
Benefits Medical, dental, and vision insurance; retirement savings plan with employer contribution; paid vacation and sick leave; 10 holidays; flexible spending account; and professional development support.
Full Job Description

The Regional Housing Partnership is seeking a Grants Coordinator to support the administration of federal, state, and local funding programs that expand affordable housing and neighborhood services. The Partnership works with municipalities, nonprofit organizations, and community-development agencies throughout the region.

The Grants Coordinator will track funding opportunities, maintain grant calendars, assist with proposal development, monitor reporting deadlines, and collect performance information from funded organizations. The position will also help prepare budgets, reimbursement requests, board materials, and compliance documentation.

The successful candidate will be highly organized and able to manage multiple deadlines. Strong writing and proofreading skills are required. Familiarity with government grants, affordable housing, community development, or nonprofit finance is preferred. Experience with spreadsheet software is required, while database or project-management experience is a plus.

A bachelor's degree in public administration, nonprofit management, business, finance, communications, or a related field is preferred, along with two years of relevant experience. Equivalent combinations of education and experience may be considered.

The Partnership has a small staff and a high degree of cross-functional collaboration. The Grants Coordinator will work closely with program staff and external partners and will frequently shift between detailed administrative work and broader project coordination.

The normal schedule is hybrid, with two remote work days per week after completion of training. Occasional evening meetings and local travel may be required to visit funded projects or attend partner events.

To apply, submit a resume, cover letter, and a short writing sample. Candidates may be asked to complete a practical exercise involving a fictional grant budget and reporting deadline.

The Regional Housing Partnership is committed to fair and inclusive hiring and provides reasonable accommodations for qualified applicants.
