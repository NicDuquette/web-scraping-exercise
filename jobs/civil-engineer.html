Civil Engineer

Lake County Department of Public Works

900 County Administration Road
Lakeview, WA 98210

Job Details

Salary Range $88,500 - $116,250 annually
Job Type Full-time
Work Setting In-person
Benefits Medical, dental, and vision insurance; state retirement plan; paid vacation and sick leave; paid professional licensing fees; 12 holidays; and optional deferred compensation.
Full Job Description

Lake County Department of Public Works is seeking a Civil Engineer to support the design, construction, and maintenance of county transportation and public-infrastructure projects. The position works with engineering staff, consultants, contractors, and other public agencies from project planning through completion.

Assignments may include roadway and drainage design, preparation and review of plans and specifications, construction inspection, cost estimates, permit coordination, and technical analysis. The engineer will also help maintain project schedules and budgets and prepare reports for department leadership and the County Commission.

Candidates should have a solid foundation in civil-engineering principles and strong technical writing and communication skills. Experience with AutoCAD, Civil 3D, GIS, or transportation-design software is preferred. Familiarity with public works construction standards and permitting processes is an advantage.

A bachelor's degree in civil engineering from an accredited program is required. Registration as a Professional Engineer is preferred but not required at the time of hire. Applicants should have at least two years of relevant engineering experience; experience with public infrastructure is helpful.

The Public Works Department manages roads, bridges, drainage facilities, and other county infrastructure. Staff spend time both in the office and in the field, and the successful candidate should be comfortable visiting active construction sites and working outdoors in varying weather conditions.

This is an in-person position based at the County Public Works facility. Local travel is frequent, and occasional early-morning site visits may be required. The position may participate in an after-hours emergency response rotation for major infrastructure incidents.

To apply, submit an application, resume, and cover letter. Candidates may be asked to participate in a technical interview and review a sample engineering problem or project scenario.

Lake County is an equal opportunity employer. Employment decisions are made without regard to protected characteristics under applicable federal, state, or local law.
