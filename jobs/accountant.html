Accountant

Port Authority of Harbor County

1 Port Administration Way
Harbor City, NJ 07001

Job Details

Salary Range $71,500 - $92,300 annually
Job Type Full-time
Work Setting Hybrid - up to two remote days per week
Benefits Medical, dental, and vision insurance; public employee retirement system; paid vacation and sick leave; 13 paid holidays; transit benefit; life insurance; and continuing-education assistance.
Full Job Description

The Port Authority of Harbor County is seeking an Accountant to support the Finance and Administration Division. The position performs professional accounting work involving reconciliations, financial reporting, accounts receivable and payable, and analysis of operating and capital transactions.

The accountant will prepare journal entries, reconcile bank and ledger accounts, review invoices, assist with monthly and year-end closing procedures, and help prepare financial statements and management reports. The position will also work with operating departments to investigate discrepancies and improve financial controls.

Candidates should have strong attention to detail and a working knowledge of accounting principles. Experience with enterprise financial systems and advanced spreadsheets is preferred. Familiarity with governmental accounting, grant accounting, or capital-project accounting is helpful but not required.

A bachelor's degree in accounting, finance, business administration, or a related field is required. At least two years of professional accounting experience is preferred. Candidates pursuing CPA certification are encouraged to apply, although a CPA license is not required for this position.

The Finance and Administration Division supports a public transportation and port organization with a diverse operating budget and substantial capital program. The work is deadline-driven, particularly around monthly and annual financial close, but the office emphasizes careful review and collaboration.

The role is hybrid, with up to two remote work days per week after the training period. Most routine work is performed during regular business hours, although occasional additional hours may be needed during month-end or year-end close.

To apply, submit an online application, resume, and cover letter. Candidates may be asked to complete a practical accounting exercise and participate in a panel interview.

The Port Authority of Harbor County is an equal opportunity employer and provides reasonable accommodations to qualified applicants.
